Namaz Shield

Privacy Policy and Support. Effective November 10, 2026.

Namaz Shield helps you pray on time. It shows prayer times, a reminder screen at prayer time, a Qibla compass, a rakat guide and a private daily record. It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

Data stored on your phone

Your settings, prayer record and nightly self-review stay on your device. We do not run a server and we never receive this information. Deleting the app deletes it.

Location

With your permission, the app uses your location to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. To do this it sends your coordinates to:

  • AlAdhan (api.aladhan.com), to get prayer times for your location.
  • BigDataCloud (api.bigdatacloud.net), to show your city name.

If you do not allow location access, the app may use ipapi (ipapi.co), which estimates your city from your internet connection. You can also pick a city by hand. These services handle requests under their own privacy policies. We do not link this information to your identity.

Screen Time (iPhone)

The app uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks to block the apps you choose during prayer time and to turn on Apple's adult website filter. Your app choices are protected by Apple and stay on your device. We cannot see which apps you choose or how you use your phone.

Website filter (Android)

On Android, the filter uses a local VPN connection that handles only website name (DNS) lookups. Those lookups are checked by the family filtering services CleanBrowsing and Cloudflare (1.1.1.3). The app does not record or send us your browsing.

Notifications

Prayer reminders are created on your phone. We do not use push notification servers.

What we do not do

We do not sell data, show ads, use analytics or track you across apps and websites.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
