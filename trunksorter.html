TrunkSorter Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

TrunkSorter helps delivery drivers load, sort and find packages in their car and guides them to each stop. It has no user accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking. Your route, packages and settings are stored only on your phone.

Camera

The camera is used to scan package labels and barcodes. Images are read on your phone and are not uploaded or stored by us.

Location

Your precise location is used while a route is running to show you on the map, to guide you turn by turn and to tell you which stop you are at, also when the app is in the background (for example while another delivery app is open). Location stays on your phone. It is never sold or shared for advertising. You can turn the GPS stop guide off in Settings or remove the permission at any time in your phone's settings.

Addresses, maps and routes

To put stops on the map and plan a route, the stop addresses and your starting point are sent to the free map services OpenStreetMap Nominatim, Photon (Komoot), OSRM and OpenFreeMap (map tiles). On iPhone, Apple Maps may be used for routes and traffic. These services receive only what they need to answer the request and are not given your name or any account details. If you tap a Google Maps or Waze button, that app or website opens with the stop's location.

Microphone and voice

The voice helper uses your phone's own speech recognition (Apple or Google) when you turn the microphone on. Your phone's maker may process the audio to turn speech into text under its own privacy policy. TrunkSorter does not record or keep audio.

Screen reading on Android (optional)

On Android you can turn on the TrunkSorter stop helper (an Accessibility service). When on, it reads the stop address shown in the delivery app you choose (never Amazon Flex) so it can show which of your packages belong to that stop. The text is read on your phone, used only for this, and not stored or sent anywhere. You can turn it off at any time in Android Settings > Accessibility.

Screen recordings and screenshots

If you give the app a screen recording or screenshot of your route list, it is read on your phone and not uploaded.

Subscription

TrunkSorter Pro is sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we do not receive your card or payment details. The app only learns whether your subscription is active.

Children

TrunkSorter is a tool for adult delivery drivers and is not directed at children.

Your choices

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your phone. Permissions can be changed at any time in your phone's settings.
